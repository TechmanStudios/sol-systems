# Dual-Track Documentation Rule: Technical Precision + Simplified English Companion

To ensure that both human collaborators and every agent across the `sol-systems` agentic network can rapidly orient themselves on the big picture while preserving 100% mathematical, physical, and code-level rigor, all major R&D walkthroughs, architectural syntheses, and discovery roadmaps must follow a **Dual-Track Companion Protocol**:

1. **Never Replace Technical Precision:**
   - Always keep the primary technical walkthroughs, research notes, and R&D roadmaps at full mathematical, topological, physical, and code-symbol precision (exact equations, operator definitions, thresholds, SHA-256 hashes, and line-linked file references).
2. **Always Provide a Simplified English Companion Alongside It:**
   - Whenever documenting a major discovery, multi-venue synthesis, or architectural milestone (in conversation walkthrough artifacts and/or `docs/companion_sessions/`), create or update a **Simplified English Companion Document** that exists **with** the technical document, **never in place of it**.
   - The Simplified English Companion must explain:
     - **The Big Picture:** What the system or engine is doing as a whole in plain, everyday English.
     - **Problem $\to$ Mechanism $\to$ Proof:** What real-world AI or engineering flaw each part solves, how our physical/topological mechanism fixes it in intuitive terms, and what our stress tests proved.
     - **Technical $\leftrightarrow$ Plain-English Translation Table:** A quick-reference table mapping formal operators/terms (e.g., $\Sigma_{ABC} = \mathcal{C}_{\tau^*} \circ \mathcal{B}_\pi \circ \mathcal{A}_\rho$, $H^1$ Cellular Sheaf Cohomology, Quotient Sheaf Compression, 30-step anti-phase quench) to their plain-English meanings.
3. **Agentic Network Onboarding & Reading Protocol:**
   - When an agent or subagent joins a task touching the SOL Engine, `SubstrateToolSandbox`, or the 5 R&D Venues, it should consult **both**:
     - [`docs/companion_sessions/FIVE_VENUE_UNIFICATION_SIMPLIFIED_ENGLISH.md`](../../docs/companion_sessions/FIVE_VENUE_UNIFICATION_SIMPLIFIED_ENGLISH.md) for fast, intuitive whole-engine orientation, and
     - [`docs/companion_sessions/NEXT_RND_VENUES_ROADMAP.md`](../../docs/companion_sessions/NEXT_RND_VENUES_ROADMAP.md) (plus the linked session reports) for exact mathematical invariants and implementation contracts.
